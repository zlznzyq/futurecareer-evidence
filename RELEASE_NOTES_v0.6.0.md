# FutureCareer v0.6.0 — Open Research Beta

## New

- Public RPLS historical labor-market layer
- 2022–2026 common-window longitudinal validation
- employment, job openings, salary, hiring and attrition dynamics
- three-window sensitivity analysis
- leave-one-SOC2-out robustness
- broad occupation-group context in the web dataset

## Research stance

The longitudinal evidence does not support treating AI exposure as a direct job-decline probability.

Results remain exploratory because RPLS occupation history is SOC2, not SOC6.

## Public promise

The open version is designed to be reproducible without a proprietary labor-market database.
