# Claims Policy

## Allowed

- “This occupation has high potential AI exposure.”
- “Observed AI use is high relative to other occupations.”
- “BLS projects employment growth of X% from 2025 to 2035.”
- “At SOC2 level, employment/posting/salary trends changed by X in the observed RPLS period.”
- “The current exploratory analysis finds an association.”

## Not allowed without new evidence

- “AI will replace X% of this occupation.”
- “This occupation is X% safe.”
- “FutureCareer predicts your career success.”
- “AI caused the observed hiring change.”
- “SOC2 history proves the exact SOC6 occupation followed the same path.”

## Rule

Public copy must distinguish:

```text
fact
derived metric
association
prediction
causal claim
```

Only the first three are central to v0.7.
