# V0.4 Research Presentation

## Core finding

The data do not support the simple rule:

> high AI exposure = poor career outlook.

Instead, the project currently supports three narrower claims.

### 1. Potential exposure and observed use are different

ILO and Anthropic overlap, but they are not interchangeable.

This motivates:

$$
Capability\rightarrow Usage\rightarrow Outcome
$$

rather than treating capability as outcome.

### 2. Occupational structure is multidimensional

Parallel analysis favors two factors over one.

The current Human Structure score remains useful as a descriptive summary, but research presentation should emphasize its dimensions.

### 3. BLS independently identifies AI-affected occupations

Occupations explicitly discussed by BLS as AI-affected score substantially higher on both ILO and Anthropic measures.

This strengthens measurement validity without implying displacement.
