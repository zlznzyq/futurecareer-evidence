# V0.3 Measurement Validation

## Result

Human Structure should **not** be treated as a proven one-dimensional construct.

Parallel analysis on the five dimensions retains **two factors**.

$$
\lambda_1=2.841,\qquad \lambda_2=1.207
$$

Both exceed the 95th-percentile eigenvalues from 2,000 random simulations.

The exploratory factor pattern is:

| Dimension | Factor 1 | Factor 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Physicality | 0.078 | -0.910 |
| Judgment | -0.875 | 0.060 |
| Interaction | -0.711 | 0.044 |
| Responsibility | -0.577 | -0.385 |
| Context | -0.983 | -0.010 |

Factor signs are arbitrary.

A practical interpretation is:

- **Decision / Social Context**: Judgment, Interaction, Responsibility, Context
- **Embodied Physicality**: Physicality, with some Responsibility cross-loading

This is a research interpretation, not a final latent-variable model.

## Reliability

For the original five-item structure:

$$
\alpha=0.715
$$

This is adequate for exploratory work, but not strong enough to justify calling the construct a universal “AI moat”.

## Discriminant validity

Human Structure is related to BLS skills, but it is not identical to them.

For the equal-weight seed:

- largest absolute correlation with a BLS skill: **0.587**
- median absolute correlation across BLS skills: **0.272**

The strongest relationship is with **Problem solving and decision making**.

This means Human Structure contains substantial skill-related information. Future versions should keep testing whether it adds information beyond general occupational skill level.

## Independent BLS AI-text validation

BLS Table 1.12 contains **48 unique SOC6 occupations** whose utilization notes explicitly mention AI/artificial intelligence.

These occupations have substantially higher AI measures:

### ILO

$$
Mean_{AI\ mention}=0.480
$$

$$
Mean_{no\ mention}=0.281
$$

$$
AUC=0.877
$$

### Anthropic

$$
Mean_{AI\ mention}=0.326
$$

$$
Mean_{no\ mention}=0.061
$$

$$
AUC=0.874
$$

This is useful independent convergent evidence that both AI measures identify occupations that BLS itself discusses as AI-affected.

It is **not** evidence that those occupations will decline.

## ILO–Anthropic gap

For the 750 occupations with both measures:

$$
Gap_o=
Percentile(ObservedAI_o)-Percentile(ILOPotential_o)
$$

Large positive gaps mean observed AI use is much higher than potential-exposure rank suggests.

Large negative gaps mean theoretical exposure is high but observed use is relatively low.

The gap is useful for case analysis, but extreme values may also reflect taxonomy and mapping error. Outliers must therefore be manually reviewed before publication.
