# UX Principles

FutureCareer should feel simple even when the research is not.

## 1. Progressive disclosure

The default view answers only three questions:

1. **机会怎么样？**
2. **AI正在怎样进入这个职业？**
3. **为什么这个职业仍然需要人？**

Technical detail appears only after the user clicks **为什么？** or **查看证据**.

## 2. Raw evidence before interpretation

Each important metric shows:

```text
plain-language label
exact score
one-sentence meaning
raw evidence
source
```

## 3. Never make users decode research vocabulary

Public label → research term:

```text
市场机会 → Market Opportunity
AI可影响程度 → Potential AI Exposure
AI实际使用 → Observed AI Usage
人的工作结构 → Human Structure
```

Research terminology remains available in methodology.

## 4. Numbers must explain themselves

A 0–100 number is accompanied by a short scale explanation.

Example:

> 88.4 / 100 — roughly the 88th percentile among comparable occupations.

## 5. Uncertainty stays visible

Use:

> 数据覆盖 92 / 100

rather than:

> 模型准确率 92%

unless accuracy has actually been measured.

## 6. SOC2 and SOC6 must never be visually confused

Historical RPLS cards must display:

> **职业大类历史趋势（SOC2）**

and explain that this is context for the occupation, not its exact SOC6 history.

## 7. Visual hierarchy

Primary:
- occupation name
- market
- AI
- human structure
- raw BLS facts

Secondary:
- composite beta index
- exposure gap
- technical validation

Tertiary:
- crosswalk details
- factor loadings
- regression diagnostics
