# Results at a Glance

## Measurement

- Research occupations: **867 SOC6**
- Human Structure complete: **786**
- Cronbach's alpha: **0.715**
- Parallel analysis: **2 factors retained**
- ILO–Anthropic overlap: **750 occupations**

## Independent AI validation

BLS explicitly mentions AI in utilization notes for **48 unique SOC6 occupations**.

| Measure | Mean: BLS AI mention | Mean: no mention | AUC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ILO potential exposure | 0.480 | 0.281 | **0.877** |
| Anthropic observed use | 0.326 | 0.061 | **0.874** |

Interpretation: both measures identify occupations that BLS independently discusses as AI-affected.

This is **not** evidence of job displacement.

## Human Structure

Parallel analysis suggests two broad components:

1. Decision / Social Context
2. Embodied Physicality

The five dimensions remain visible because the construct is not yet a final one-factor scale.

## Main research lesson

> AI exposure is evidence about technological reach, not a direct forecast of occupational decline.
